FAQ

My shop is big. How do I add all my products?

It’s much quicker than you think. Oja already has thousands of ready-made products. You search, select what you stock, then enter your quantity and prices. You don’t type product names one by one. If you can’t find something, add it yourself.

Prefer a hand? Book a one-on-one setup. Our team visits your shop on a day that suits you and sets everything up with you.

Will Oja tell me how much profit I’m really making?

Yes. Enter what each product costs you, and Oja shows your profit on every sale. Add your expenses (rent, fuel, NEPA, salaries) and it shows your net profit, the money you actually keep, for any day, week, month or year. Rent you pay for a whole year is spread across the months it covers, so one big payment never ruins a month’s numbers.

Many of my customers buy on credit. Can Oja keep track?

Yes. Sell on credit from the same screen you use for any sale, with the customer’s name and what they pay now. Oja keeps the list of who owes you, how much, and who is overdue. You record each payment as it comes in, even in small parts. No more debt notebook.

Can I give my staff access without giving away everything?

Yes. Each staff member gets their own login, and every sale is recorded under their name. Your expenses and credit records stay with owners and managers.

I have more than one shop. Can Oja handle that?

Yes. Add every shop to one account. Each shop keeps its own stock, sales and reports, and you switch between them in a tap.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your records are safe. They’re saved securely online, not only on your phone. Sign in on a new phone and carry on where you stopped.
