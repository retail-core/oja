Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Oja is operated by Lynx Inc, Edo, Nigeria ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Oja mobile app and website, how we use it, and your rights. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name, email address, phone number, business name and password.

Business data you enter. Products, stock levels, prices, sales, expenses, stores, staff accounts, and debt records. Debt records may include your customers' names, phone numbers and balances.

Images. Product photos you take or upload.

Subscription information. When paid plans are available, your plan and payment status. Card details are collected by our payment provider, not by us.

Device information. Device type, operating system, app version and IP address, recorded in our server logs to keep the service working and secure.

Device permissions.

  • Camera and photos: to take or choose product images you upload. Used only when you choose these features.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to your receipt printer.

You can turn these off in your device settings. Features that need them will stop working.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and provide the app's features
  • To verify your identity and send one-time codes and account emails
  • To process payments for paid plans
  • To provide support and, where you request it, set up your shop
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud
  • To meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Your business and customer data

The business data you enter belongs to you. We process it only to provide the service to you. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record your own customers' information in Oja.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with providers that help us run Oja:

  • Paystack, to process payments for paid plans
  • Postmark, to send account and verification emails
  • Cloudflare, to store product images
  • Our hosting provider, to run our servers

They may process data only to provide these services to us. We may also disclose data when the law requires it or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. Storage outside Nigeria

Our servers and some of our providers are outside Nigeria. By using Oja, you understand your data may be stored and processed in other countries. We take steps to ensure it receives protection consistent with the NDPA.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and data, email llcvatex@gmail.com from your registered email address. We complete deletion within 5 working days. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, such as payment records.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational measures, including access controls and encrypted connections, to protect your data. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the regulator as the NDPA requires.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email llcvatex@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

Oja is a business app for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any update on this page and change the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email.
